Introduction

As the World Wide Web grew more and more important in our daily lives, it is important to understand the technology behind it. One of the Most Important aspects of the web are web servers. Web servers are computers that store and deliver web pages to users when they request them. Which out websites, Most of the internet experience we know today will not exist. In this project, we will learn the history of web servers and two of the most important web servers today.
